Privacy

Mnemo collects nothing. There is no account, no server and no analytics. The app doesn’t send your poems, your marks or your voice anywhere.

What stays on your phone

The poems you add, the marks you make, and when each stanza is due again are kept on your phone, in the app’s own storage. Your phone’s own backup (iCloud or Google) includes them, if you have that turned on. Nobody else can see them, us included.

Listening

When you recite aloud, Mnemo asks to use the microphone and speech recognition. Your phone recognises the words itself, with recognition that runs on the device, and Mnemo lines them up with the poem. The sound is thrown away straight after.

If you choose keep the latest under your recitals in settings, Mnemo keeps one recording per poem, on your phone only, so you can hear it back. clear recordings removes them all. Recordings are never in an export file. You can always type instead of speaking.

Export

An export file holds your poems, your marks and how each one is coming along. It goes wherever you choose to send or save it, and nowhere else.

Children

Mnemo is fine for any age. Since it collects nothing, it collects nothing from children either.
