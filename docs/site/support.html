Mnemo

A page that slowly forgets a poem, so that you learn it by heart. You mark what you hear, fade the words, and say it by heart.

Something wrong, or a question

Write to CONTACT. Say which phone you have, and what you did just before it went wrong.

Common questions

Where are my poems? Only on your phone. Use export in settings to keep a copy, or to move them to a new phone with import.

It doesn’t hear me. Check that Mnemo may use the microphone and speech recognition, in your phone’s settings. Older phones can’t recognise speech on the device; there you can type instead.

A word I said is shown as missed. Tap it to say you did say it. Old or rare words are easy to mishear.

A long line breaks in two. It is still one line. The rest turns over, indented, as a printed book does it. Turn the phone sideways for a wider page.

Privacy: Mnemo collects nothing.
